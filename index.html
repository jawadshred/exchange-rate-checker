Exchange Rate Checker

Enter the Google exchange rate and the shop's exchange rate, then check how far apart they are.

Enter values and click the button.
Formula: (Google Rate − Shop Rate) / Google Rate × 100

What the result means

Less than 2.5% means the shop rate is close to the Google rate.

Between 2.5% and 3% means the shop rate is moderately different.

More than 3% means the shop rate is significantly different from Google.

This helps you quickly judge whether the exchange shop rate is reasonable.
