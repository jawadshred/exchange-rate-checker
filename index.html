Exchange Rate Checker

Exchange rates are fetched automatically from a free public API. Change the currencies below and the latest rate will populate the market rate field.

Formula: (Market Rate − Shop Rate) / Market Rate × 100
Enter values and click the button.
This uses the free, keyless Frankfurter API to fetch the latest rate for the selected currency pair.

What the result means

Less than 2.5% means the shop rate is close to the market rate.

Between 2.5% and 3% means the shop rate is moderately different.

More than 3% means the shop rate is significantly different from the market rate.

This helps you quickly judge whether the exchange shop rate is reasonable.
